Narwi — three marks

Each concept is shown as a lockup with نروي and Narwi, large, as an app icon, and at 48, 32 and 16 pixels — in light, dark and a single colour. Colours are the Illuminated palette: lapis, red ink, gold.

Needs your call

  1. Pick one mark to refine into the full logo set. You can also ask to combine ideas.
A

Nūn

نrecommended

The first letter of نروي, drawn as its bowl and dot. The bowl is a cup — نروي, "we quench". The red dot is the scribe's ink and a drop falling into it. And the letter carries the oldest image of learning in the Arabic tradition: ن والقلم وما يسطرون — "Nūn. By the pen and what they write."

Works
  • The only one that is also Arabic script — it says نروي before you read it
  • Three meanings in two shapes: letter, cup, ink drop
  • Crisp at 16px; two shapes, nothing thin
Watch
  • As sketched, it can read as a smiley face. The refinement fixes this by drawing a true calligraphic nūn: a deeper bowl that thickens at the bottom and tapers to a point at each tip, with a diamond-shaped pen dot instead of a round one
  • Simple shapes are easy to resemble — needs a trademark look-up
  • Latin readers see a bowl and a dot, not a letter — the wordmark carries "Narwi"
A2

Nūn, refined

نnew

The same idea drawn as a scribe would write it. The bowl is one pen stroke: thick at the base, thinner up the sides, with flat, angled ends where the reed pen lifted. The dot is the diamond a reed pen leaves when pressed once. Flat pen-cut ends stop it reading as a crescent; the deeper, heavier bowl stops it reading as a smile.

A next to A2 · same sizes

Changed from A
  • Bowl: uniform line → one calligraphic stroke, heavier at the base
  • Ends: round → flat pen cuts, the right end higher, like a written ن
  • Dot: circle → diamond pen dot
Still to check
  • Whether you still see a face — your eye is the test here
  • Trademark look-up for the final shape
B

Isnād

إسناد

The chain of transmission: author, teacher, you. Two lapis links that came before, and a red one that is the learner receiving — نروي، فتتلقّى.

Works
  • Tells the tagline as a picture
  • Reads clearly at every size
Watch
  • Three linked dots is a common "network / share" icon — generic
  • Says nothing Arabic or classical on its own
C

Drop

قطرة

نروي as "we quench": a drop of water, with a red curve inside that reads as a smile, a ripple, or the curve of a voice.

Works
  • Instantly recognisable shape; warm and friendly
  • Strong as an app icon
Watch
  • Water drops belong to utilities, water brands and health apps
  • Loses the learning story — the drop needs the name to explain it
