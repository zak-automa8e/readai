**Name: Narwi · نروي** ("we narrate", also "we quench"). Chosen by the owner after round 3.

**Tagline: نروي، فتتلقّى كما تلقَّوا** — *We narrate, you receive, as they did.*

## Round 3 — coined from Arabic roots

Owner 2026-10-16: rejected Mihbara and Talaqqi (sound / feel, too obscure); `mustamli.com` is not theirs;
direction = names coined from familiar Arabic roots; Arabic written by Claude, no Gemini pass.

**Recommendation: Uloomi · علومي · *oo-LOO-mee*.** Runner-up: **Narwi · نروي · *NAR-wee***.

**Uloomi** — "my sciences". علوم is a word every Arab knows, and the golden age is remembered for exactly
that: the sciences — astronomy beside grammar, medicine beside fiqh. The "-i" makes it personal: *my* sciences,
my path through them. It stretches to every medium and subject, past books and past audio. In Gulf speech
"وش علومك؟" means "what's your news?", so it also sounds warm and everyday.

- Domains: `uloomi.app` free · `uloomi.ai` free · `uloomi.com` is a Danish board-game publisher (other space,
  other country — low conflict, possible later purchase).
- App Store (Saudi): no app with the name.
- Watch: in South Asia "Uloom" evokes Darul Uloom seminaries — minor for an Arab audience. Latin variants
  Ulumi / Oloomi exist.

**Narwi** — "we narrate", and also "we quench" (يروي العطش). The verb of every chain of transmission (رواه),
in the first person plural: we pass it on, together — the pay-it-forward model in one word.

- Domains: `narwi.app` free · `narwi.ai` free · `narwi.com` registered, no site (worth an offer).
- App Store (Saudi): none.
- Watch: **`yarwy.com` (يَروي, Yarwy) is a live Arabic audiobook platform since May 2025** — same verb, same
  space; Narwi sits close to it. Leans to narration, so it stretches less than Uloomi.

## Rounds 1–2 recommendation (rejected by owner)

**Mihbara · محبرة · the inkwell.** Runner-up: **Talaqqi · تلقّي**.

Mihbara is the only name of 18 checked with no product on it anywhere, a free `.ai`, and a free `.com` variant. It
is a word Arabs know but nobody uses day to day, so it can be owned. The inkwell went with the student to every
lesson for life — *"with the inkwell, until the grave"* (attributed to Imam Ahmad) — and ink draws, so the name
carries images and video later. Its weakness: it names the student's tool, not the practice, and it leans to
writing while the product leads with listening.

Talaqqi names the practice exactly — receiving knowledge mouth-to-ear from a teacher — and `talaqqi.com` can be
bought for $3,995. Its weaknesses: English speakers stumble on "qq", and it reads as Quran tutoring (a small
Quran-teacher site holds `talaqqi.app`).

Before committing: Gemini dialect check (blocked on `GEMINI_API_KEY`), manual social-handle check, and a trademark
search in Saudi Arabia (SAIP) and the target markets by a registrar or lawyer.

## Shortlist

### Mihbara · محبرة · *mih-BAA-ra* — recommended

The inkwell. Students carried it to every circle; Imam Ahmad, asked how long he would keep seeking knowledge, is
reported to have said *"with the inkwell, until the grave"* (مع المحبرة إلى المقبرة). Ink writes, and ink draws.

- Domains: `mihbara.ai` free · `mehbara.com` free · `mihbara.com` registered since 2020, unused (default server
  page) — worth an offer · `mihbara.app` registered, empty.
- Conflicts: none found in Latin or Arabic, App Store (Saudi + US) or the web. LOW.
- Watch: ح gives two spellings (Mihbara / Mehbara); the Latin form looks a little like *mihrab*; it leans to
  writing. Quote the saying as "attributed to Imam Ahmad" — the chain was not checked.

### Talaqqi · تلقّي · *ta-LAK-kee* — runner-up

Receiving knowledge mouth-to-ear from a teacher — the defining method of traditional learning, and literally
what an AI voice reading a book to you does.

- Domains: `talaqqi.ai` free · `talaqqi.com` for sale, $3,995 buy-now (Afternic) · `gettalaqqi.com`,
  `talaqqiapp.com` free · `talaqqi.app` held by a young Quran-teacher / duas site (registered 2026-03, "app coming
  soon").
- Conflicts: MyTalaqqi (Malaysia, 0 ratings) and two small Arabic "تلقي" Quran-teaching apps. MEDIUM.
- Watch: "qq" trips English speakers; strongly tied to Quran recitation, so it may read as Quran tutoring.

### Mustamli · مستملي · *mus-TAM-lee* — best story, blocked

In large dictation sessions, the *mustamlī* repeated the teacher's words aloud so the farthest rows could hear
and write (al-Samʿānī, *Adab al-imlāʾ wa-l-istimlāʾ*). Carrying the teacher's voice to everyone is this product.

- **`mustamli.com` was registered on 2026-10-03 (Cloudflare) and serves a live Arabic web app titled مستملي —
  "اقرأ واستمع، كلمةً بكلمة" ("read and listen, word by word").** Same space. Built with Compose Multiplatform,
  not this codebase. `mustamli.app`, `mustamli.ai` free.
- Viable only if that site is the owner's.

### Rihla · رحلة · *RIH-la*

The journey for knowledge — the owner's own framing.

- Domains: only `getrehla.com` free (spelling doesn't match) · `rihla.com` for sale at $50,000 · `rihla.study`
  registered 2026-07-18, a live Arabic site titled "Rihla — رحلة" (purpose unverified) · every other variant
  checked is taken.
- Conflicts: 14 "Rihla" apps in the Saudi App Store (travel, prayer); in Arabic it is the everyday word for "trip".
- Keep it as a product word instead — a book's learning path could be its *rihla*.

### Imla · إملاء · *im-LAA*

Dictation sessions. Clean conflicts (none found), but only `imlaa.ai` / `imlaa.app` are free, and to most Arabs
it is the school subject "spelling" — the opposite of "Curious, not academic".

### Adab · أدب · *A-dab*

Literature, and the etiquette of learning. Everyday word; `adab.com` is a live Arabic-literature encyclopedia;
only `adabapp.com` free. Generic.

### Benchmark: Khabeer · خبير

Every exact domain taken; 5+ unrelated Khabeer / Khabir apps; names the expert, not the practice. Fails.

## Method and limits

- Domains: registry lookups (RDAP) on 2026-10-16; a nonsense control returned 404 on .com, .app, .ai. `.io`
  could not be checked (its RDAP answers 404 even for google.io).
- App Store: Apple's public search API, Saudi and US stores, Latin and Arabic spellings (round 2). Google Play:
  web search only.
- Not checked: social handles (x.com and Instagram answer to any handle), trademarks (WIPO / TMview not
  queryable), Arabic dialect meanings (Gemini key not set).
- Sources: `My Vault/Projects/ReadAI Brand/Name Screen A.md`, `B.md`, `C.md`, `D.md`.
