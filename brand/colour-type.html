Brand identity · Story 3 of 6

Three directions for Narwi

Each one is built from the colours of the manuscripts — lapis, red ink, gold, brass, ink black — and none uses the green, tan or orange competitors already own. Same screens in all three, so you compare only colour and type. Switch Light / Dark at the top.

The small chips under each palette are live contrast checks against WCAG AA (4.5 : 1 for text).

Needs your call

  1. Pick one direction — or name the parts you like from each (e.g. "colour of 1, type of 2").
  2. Anything that still feels dull, say so — that is exactly what this round is for.
1

Illuminated

المُذهَّب

The illuminated manuscript page: lapis blue that carries the brand, red ink that marks what is being read, gold kept for ornament. Classical type — Amiri and Fraunces — over a clean modern interface in Readex Pro. Rich and confident; the closest to "a golden age, now".

2

Rubric

الحِبر الأحمر

Scribes wrote in black ink and marked headings in red — the rubric. Here that is the whole system: ink black, paper white, one hot vermilion. One modern family, Alexandria, for both scripts. Sharp, editorial, the boldest and most modern of the three.

3

Astrolabe

الأسطرلاب

The golden age of science: the observatory at night and the brass of the astrolabe. Deep sea-night teal with brass, softer corners, El Messiri for headings and Rubik for the interface. Calm and curious; it speaks to the "science" half of the idea.
