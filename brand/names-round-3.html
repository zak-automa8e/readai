Your brief: sounds good said aloud, needs no story to explain it, built from roots every Arab knows.

Recommended

Uloomi علومي

oo-LOO-mee · my sciences

The golden age is remembered for its sciences — astronomy beside grammar, medicine beside fiqh. Uloomi makes them personal: my sciences, my path through them. It reaches past books and past audio to everything you'll learn here. And in Gulf speech وش علومك؟ means "what's your news?" — so it sounds warm and everyday too.

uloomi.app and uloomi.ai are freeuloomi.com is a Danish board-game publisher — another business, another country
No app on itSaudi App Store search returns nothing named Uloomi
Clear on first hearingعلوم needs no explanation; the "-i" is the brand
Narwiنروي

Runner-up. "We narrate" — and "we quench" (نروي العطش). The verb of every chain of transmission, said together: we pass it on. narwi.app and narwi.ai are free. But yarwy.com (يَروي) is a live Arabic audiobook platform on the same verb, and it leans to narration.

Needs your call

  1. Pick one: Uloomi, Narwi, or neither.
  2. Before you buy: look up @uloomi on X, Instagram and TikTok yourself, and get a trademark search in Saudi Arabia (SAIP). Neither can be checked from here.
