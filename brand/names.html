Recommended

Mihbara محبرة

mih-BAA-ra · the inkwell

The student carried it to every lesson, for life. Imam Ahmad, asked how long he would keep seeking knowledge, is reported to have said: with the inkwell, until the grave. Ink writes — and ink draws, so the name grows into images and video.

Nothing on itNo app, company or product found — Latin or Arabic, App Store Saudi + US, the web
mihbara.ai is freeand so is mehbara.com; mihbara.com is registered but unused since 2020 — worth an offer
OwnableEvery Arab knows the word; nobody uses it day to day
Talaqqiتلقّي

Runner-up. Names the practice exactly — receiving knowledge mouth-to-ear from a teacher. talaqqi.com is for sale at $3,995 and talaqqi.ai is free. But "qq" trips English speakers, and it reads as Quran tutoring.

Needs your call

  1. Pick one: Mihbara, Talaqqi, or neither (I run a third round).
  2. Is mustamli.com yours? It was registered on 3 Oct 2026 and runs an Arabic "read and listen, word by word" app. If it's yours, Mustamli has the best story of all and jumps to the top.
  3. Before you buy anything: a Gemini dialect check (needs GEMINI_API_KEY), a look at the social handles yourself, and a trademark search in Saudi Arabia (SAIP) and your other markets.

The shortlist

Mihbaraمحبرة

mih-BAA-ra · the inkwell

recommended11 / 14

The student's inkwell, carried to every circle for life — "with the inkwell, until the grave" (مع المحبرة إلى المقبرة), attributed to Imam Ahmad. Ink writes and ink draws.

Domains

  • mihbara.aifree
  • mehbara.comfreespelling variant
  • mihbara.comregistered 2020, unuseddefault server page — make an offer
  • mihbara.appregistered, empty

Conflicts

None found — Latin or Arabic, App Store (Saudi + US), the web.

Watch

  • It names the student's tool, not the practice, and leans to writing while the product leads with listening
  • ح gives two Latin spellings (Mihbara / Mehbara); the Latin form looks a little like mihrab
  • Quote the saying as "attributed to Imam Ahmad" — reported via his son Ṣāliḥ; the chain wasn't checked
Talaqqiتلقّي

ta-LAK-kee · receiving knowledge from a teacher

runner-up10 / 14

The defining method of traditional learning: the student receives the text mouth-to-ear from a teacher. An AI voice reading a book to you is literally that.

Domains

  • talaqqi.aifree
  • talaqqi.comfor sale · $3,995Afternic buy-now, offers allowed
  • gettalaqqi.comfree
  • talaqqi.apptakenyoung Quran-teacher / duas site, "app coming soon"

Conflicts

MyTalaqqi (Malaysia, 0 ratings) and two small Arabic "تلقي" Quran-teaching apps. Medium.

Watch

  • "qq" trips English speakers; some will write Talaqi
  • Tied to Quran recitation — may read as a Quran tutoring app, while ours is turath
Mustamliمستملي

mus-TAM-lee · the one who carries the teacher's voice

blocked unless it's yours10 / 14

In large dictation sessions the mustamlī repeated the teacher's words aloud so the farthest rows could hear and write (al-Samʿānī, Adab al-imlāʾ wa-l-istimlāʾ). Carrying the teacher's voice to everyone is this product.

Domains

  • mustamli.comlive, same spaceregistered 3 Oct 2026 · "مستملي — اقرأ واستمع، كلمةً بكلمة"
  • mustamli.appfree
  • mustamli.aifree
Rihlaرحلة

The journey for knowledge — your own framing. But rihla.com is $50,000, only getrehla.com is free, a live Arabic "Rihla — رحلة" site has held rihla.study since July, and in Arabic it's the everyday word for "trip".

Keep it as a product word instead — a book's learning path could be its rihla.

Imlaإملاء

Dictation sessions. Clean of conflicts, but only imlaa.ai / imlaa.app are free — and to most Arabs it is the school subject "spelling". Drills, not wonder.

Adabأدب

Literature, and the etiquette of learning. An everyday word; adab.com is a live Arabic-literature encyclopedia; only adabapp.com is free.

Khabeerخبير

The benchmark. Every exact domain is taken, five or more unrelated Khabeer / Khabir apps exist, and it names the expert, not the practice.
